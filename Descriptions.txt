Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two
armchairs. Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along
with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting. You
can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good
vacuuming.

Doorways lead west to your father's office, north to your
bedroom, and east to the kitchen.
#
Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here
when you were six. A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next
to your twin bed. Posters of various anime characters cover one
wall. A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentience,
occupies the far corner.

The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of
clothes. You can return south to the living room.
#
Office
A converted office was once a bedroom, with beige walls and a 
carpeted floor. It has one desk, a computer, an office chair, 
and two opposing chairs.

The door to the living room is to the east.
#
Kitchen
The kitchen sits in the corner of the house. between the living 
room and the garage. It has been redone with modern appliances like 
a electric stovetop. It has an island for everyone to eat at.

You can return to the living room to the west, or head south to
the garage.
#
Garage
The garage is dirty and an old unfinished project car takes up most 
of the space. Oil and grease marks stain the floor and tools are
spread halfhazardly across the workbench.

The kitchen is to the north.
#
Attic
The attic is filled with dusty old boxes and holiday decorations. One 
single lightbulb illumonates the entire floor from the middle of the
room. 

You can climb back down to the bedroom.
