The Castle Grounds
The castle stands above a large grassy area with a stone path. The path leads west to the stables and south to the living quarters.

You can go west to the stables or south to the living quarters.
#
The Stables
The stables smell of hay and wood, and a horse stands in its stall. Supplies like food and riding gear are stored along the walls.

The castle grounds are to the east, the dining hall is to the south, and the spire is upstairs.
#
The Living Quarters
The living quarters have a long table, a few chairs, and personal belongings around the room. The stone walls make the room feel cool and quiet.

You can go north to the castle grounds or west to the dining hall.
#
The Dining Hall
A long table and heavy chairs fill the dining hall. Candles and old banners decorate the stone walls.

The stables are to the north, the living quarters are to the east, and the throne room is to the south.
#
The Spire
The spire is a tall, narrow room where the wind blows through the open windows. A small platform offers a view of the castle grounds below.

You can go back down the stairs to the stables.
#
The Throne Room
The throne room has stone walls, dark banners, and a large throne at the far end. A book and a crown sit nearby, lit by sunlight from the high windows.

You can go north to the dining hall.
